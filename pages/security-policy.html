Security Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026. How to report a security vulnerability, and how we handle it.

🔒If you've found a genuine security vulnerability, we want to know. This page tells you how to report it and what to expect from us.

1. Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability affecting Oz Indie Collective (our website, platform, or associated infrastructure), please report it to us privately before disclosing it publicly.

Email security@ozindiecollective.com.au with as much detail as you can provide:

  • A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
  • Steps to reproduce it, including any affected URLs, endpoints or account states
  • Any proof-of-concept code, screenshots or logs that help us understand the issue
  • Your assessment of severity, if you have one

2. What to Expect From Us

  • Acknowledgement of your report within 3 business days
  • An initial assessment of severity and validity within 7 business days
  • Regular updates as we work through remediation, until the issue is resolved
  • Credit in our acknowledgements (see below), if you'd like it — we're also happy to keep you anonymous

3. Scope

In scope:

  • ozindiecollective.com.au and its subdomains
  • Our public API and Edge Functions used by the platform
  • Authentication, authorisation and account/session handling
  • Data exposure issues, including any way member data could be accessed without proper authorisation

Out of scope:

  • Third-party services we integrate with (e.g. Stripe, our email provider) — please report those directly to the provider
  • Social engineering, physical attacks, or denial-of-service testing against our infrastructure
  • Issues that require unlikely user interaction combined with a non-standard or outdated browser
  • Missing security headers or best-practice suggestions with no demonstrated exploit (we still welcome these as feedback via our contact form, just not as a formal vulnerability report)

4. Ground Rules

We ask that you:

  • Give us a reasonable amount of time to investigate and fix an issue before disclosing it publicly
  • Avoid accessing, modifying or deleting data that isn't your own
  • Avoid any action that could degrade the platform's availability for other members
  • Only interact with test accounts you control, or your own account, when demonstrating an issue

Good-faith security research that follows this policy will not result in legal action from us.

5. Our Approach to Data Security

All member data is protected by row-level security policies at the database layer, not just application-level checks. Privileged operations (such as account deletion or administrative actions) are handled through authenticated, server-side functions rather than being exposed directly to the browser. Secrets and API keys are never stored in client-side code. All traffic to the platform is served over HTTPS.

6. Acknowledgements

We're grateful to researchers who help keep Oz Indie Collective and our members safe. With permission, we'll list contributors who report a valid vulnerability here.

7. Contact

Security reports: security@ozindiecollective.com.au

General enquiries: use our contact form instead — this address is for security reports only.
